Terms of Use

The Short Version

Every page of Life is yours to use, for free. Everything you write and keep in Life stays yours. Life Pro is optional: it adds a few extras and takes the ads away, and you can stop it any time.

Apple's Licence

You get Life from the App Store, so Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies to it. The lines on this page add to that agreement and don't take anything away from it.

What You Keep in Life

Your notes, journal, letters, photos and everything else you add belong to you. Life keeps them on your iPhone, and I never receive them. Because they live on your phone, keep a backup — an iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer is enough.

Life Pro

  • Life Pro comes as a monthly or a yearly subscription. The store shows every price in your currency before you buy.
  • The yearly plan can start with a free week. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the week ends, the year begins and is charged. Any unused part of a free week ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • A subscription renews by itself unless you turn renewal off at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that.
  • You can manage or cancel it any time in Life, in Settings → Life Pro → Manage Subscription, or in your phone's Settings under your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays on until the end of the time you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • If Pro ends, nothing you made is deleted. The extras stop and the ads come back.

Ads

Without Life Pro, Life shows a few ads from Google, each one marked “Ad”. They are never full screen, and never on the pages you write in. The Privacy Policy says how they work.

They belong to their owners and to the people who wrote them, and Life shows them as those services share them. They can be incomplete or wrong — and so can the food and weather words Apple Intelligence writes from them — and I can't promise they will always be there.

Names, titles, posters and logos belong to their owners. Life isn't made, endorsed or certified by any of these services. Where to Watch opens JustWatch's website, and what happens there is up to JustWatch.
